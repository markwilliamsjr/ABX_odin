# Roadmap 1.6 — Verify the slice

Status: done
Roadmap: ROADMAP.org 1.6

"Return queue mechanics", "Return scope", "Flee animation phase", "Vet review

Steps only, no code.

## Scope

Exercise the level/wave slice built in 1.1–1.5 and record evidence that seeded
generation is reproducible and that flee, return, roster, and transition behavior
matches the documented rules. It does not fix the remaining non-determinism
(Phase 2), add unit tests (1.7), or run the vet gate (1.8). There is no
player-visible behavior change; the only source edits are temporary and reverted.

## Decisions already settled

- Determinism covers *decisions*, not motion. Replay checks compare generated
  structure, not gameplay outcomes. (DECISIONS "Determinism")
- Seeds chain per parent; uniqueness is per `(parent, category, number)` triple,
  now documented as eight triples with the failed-spawn retry caveat. (DECISIONS
  "Seeding"; updated 2026-11-23)
- Level 1 is structurally identical across seeds; variance ramps from level 3.
  (DECISIONS "Early-level determinism")
- The roster is FIFO, delay-one, capped at `MAX_ENEMIES`, and survives
  transitions. (DECISIONS "Return queue mechanics", "Return scope")
- The flee phase is zero-length in 1.3; a phase kill waits for the animation.
  (DECISIONS "Flee animation phase"; ROADMAP 1.6)
- Vet stays deferred to gate 1.8. (DECISIONS "Vet review cadence")
- Verification uses temporary edits per check, not a committed harness; raw logs
  go under `/tmp/opencode/`, only conclusions go in docs. (Settled with developer
  2026-11-23)
- The outstanding 1.4 and 1.5 runtime checks in `SESSION.md` are folded into this
  step as a labeled block. (Settled with developer 2026-11-23)

## Before you start

- Confirm `odin check src` is clean and capture that as the baseline.
- Record source hashes of every file in `src/` before any temporary edit, and
  re-check after each revert. Temporary edits must never reach a commit.
- Run headlessly under the dummy SDL video driver; capture logs per check under
  `/tmp/opencode/1.6/` with a descriptive filename.
- `game.config.level` is the **starting** level only. `--level N` generates level
  N directly from `master_seed`, so structural checks need not play through
  earlier levels.
- `wave_init` logs no seed, count, threshold, or formation fields today.
  Observing them requires temporary log lines; do not leave them in source.
- The verification mechanism is deliberately not a committed `--verify` mode.
  If a check needs to be repeated often, hand it to 1.7's test seam rather than
  growing a harness here.

## Steps

### A. Seed derivation audit — `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `src/level.odin` read-only

- **A1.** DECISIONS "Seeding" was updated from seven to eight triples to include
  the 1.4 species draw, and records the retry caveat. The eight `derive_seed`
  call sites in `level.odin` are the evidence; no source change is needed.
- **A2.** Confirm each triple has exactly one purpose and no two share a parent,
  category, and number with different meanings. The species triple's `1 +
  spawn_index` keeps it clear of the jitter triple's `0`.
- **A3.** Capture the audit result to `/tmp/opencode/1.6/seed-triples.txt`.

Per-step check: eight call sites in `level.odin`, eight documented triples, no
unexplained duplicate.

### B. Structural checks — temporary logs in `src/level.odin`, `wave_init`

- **B1.** Temporarily log at wave init: level number, wave index, wave seed,
  `enemy_count`, `threshold_permille`, entry point, and formation positions.
- **B2.** Run `--seed S --level N` for N in {1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 11, 16, 20, 21, 26}
  and at least three fixed seeds; capture one log per (N, S).
- **B3.** Level 1: one wave, five enemies, top-center entry, for every seed.
- **B4.** Wave-count ranges: compare observed counts with the block table in
  DECISIONS "Early-level determinism"; level 26 must draw 10 waves without an
  `int_range` panic.
- **B5.** Seed sensitivity: levels 1–2 near-identical across seeds, level 3+
  observably different in wave count, entry point, and formation.
- **B6.** Revert the log lines and confirm source hashes.

Per-step check: a (N, S) capture exists for every combination, and the level-1
and range claims are backed by a quoted line rather than an impression.

### C. Replay and flee/return determinism — temporary forced crossing

- **C1.** Run the same seed twice; compare the generated-structure logs from B2
  plus spawn logs byte-for-byte.
- **C2.** Force a crossing at a fixed frame in both runs; compare threshold,
  buff category and fraction per slot, roster push order, and return schedule.
- **C3.** Confirm flee and return add no wall-clock-derived *decisions* to those
  fields. Motion and `flee_start_time` are log-only and belong to Phase 2.

Per-step check: the two runs' generated fields match exactly; a differing line
must be traced to motion or the clock before it is called a bug.

### D. Roster and transition — temporary preload and deferred finish

- **D1.** Flee on the last wave of level 1; confirm the entry survives the
  transition and returns in level 2's first wave with species and buffs intact,
  bullets cleared, and `wave_index == 0`.
- **D2.** Preload the roster to `MAX_ENEMIES`, then force extra flees; confirm
  drop-oldest and that overflow stays queued for the following wave.
- **D3.** Temporarily delay `wave_flee_finish` a few frames; confirm the wave
  does not advance while `flee_phase_active` is true, and advances on the finish
  frame.
- **D4.** Leave the phase-kill check unchecked and annotate it in ROADMAP 1.6
  with a pointer to the animation work / Phase 6.

Per-step check: the deferred-finish run shows advancement only on the finish
frame; the overflow entry returns in the next wave rather than being lost.

### E. Folded 1.4 and 1.5 runtime checks

- **E1.** Species unlock by level: 1 Strep only; 6 adds Staph; 11 adds E. coli;
  16 adds Pseudomonas.
- **E2.** Every fresh spawn has a finite nonzero `entry_path_length` and reaches
  `.Holding`; every species dives and returns without stale positions; E. coli
  zigzags and bounces off edges; Pseudomonas sweeps its curve.
- **E3.** Buff effects: Hardy raises `max_health` (five block-4 Hardy rolls give
  exactly `base * 6`); Frenzied raises Strep entry and dive speed; Resistant
  reduces damage with a floor of 1; Biofilm absorbs exactly its rolled hit count
  and resets on spawn/return; no Biofilm absorbs nothing; a low early roll still
  absorbs one hit.
- **E4.** A second dive in the same life uses a new return path, not the first.
- **E5.** Same seed twice: identical species per slot, entry positions, buff
  fractions, and dives.
- **E6.** Transition: exactly one `transition start` for level 1 and one
  `transition end next=2`; level 2 starts with a fresh pool; simulation is
  suspended during the hold; overlay ramps to its dim alpha; `--level 16`
  advances to 17 without re-reading the CLI; `--debug` transitions normally;
  wave resolution, flee, roster tick, and logging are unchanged until the
  transition starts.

Per-step check: each E-check has a captured log or observation; anything not
observed stays listed as outstanding rather than being marked passing.

## Outcome

Verified end to end with a temporary instrumented build under the dummy SDL
video driver. All temporary edits were reverted, source hashes matched the
baseline, and `odin check src` stayed clean. All nine roadmap boxes are ticked;
the phase-kill half of one box is annotated as deferred to the flee animation
(Phase 6) because the phase is zero-length in 1.3.

What the checks showed:

- Seed derivation audited statically: 8 call sites, 8 documented triples, one
  purpose each. DECISIONS "Seeding" now states the invariant in terms of purpose
  and records the failed-spawn retry; ARCHITECTURE.md was aligned to match.
- Same-seed replay (seed 7, level 3, forced flee every wave, 3000 frames): all
  365 generated-field lines were byte-identical. The only four differences were
  `[verify] forced flee: frame=` timings, off by one frame — pacing, not a
  decision.
- Seed sensitivity, level-1 shape, and wave-count ranges matched the docs:
  level 1 was one wave of five at top-center, levels 1–2 stayed pinned, level 3
  varied across seeds, and level 26 drew 10 waves without an `int_range` panic.
- Flee/return determinism, roster survival across the transition, deferred
  finish gating, drop-oldest at cap, species unlocks, and the four buff effects
  all held. The folded 1.4 and 1.5 runtime checks passed.

Deviations and findings:

- Verification used temporary per-check edits rather than a committed harness,
  as planned. Raw logs are in `/tmp/opencode/1.6/` and are not part of the repo.
- `buff_total` shadowed `present` with `present := true` inside its loop, so the
  Biofilm one-hit floor never fired. The developer fixed it afterward to
  `present = true`. A temporary self-test line printed a wrong Resistant
  expectation label — 400 permille → 6 damage is correct behavior, not a defect.
- The full-pool spawn-failure path and a mid-phase kill remain untested at
  runtime; both need a future temporary edit or the animation work.
- No code was changed by this plan. The only repo change was the fix above,
  committed by the developer after verification closed.
