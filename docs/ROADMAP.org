** Phase 3B — Scrolling layered background

Inserted after Phase 3A and before Phase 4. Two layers of 32×32 panels scroll
downward at different speeds to imply forward motion. Each panel loops through
3–4 frames. Panel choice, orientation, and the per-level subset are seeded. The
front layer is sparse so the back layer reads through it, and the pattern is
periodic so vertical scrolling wraps without a special seam. Presentation only:
no gameplay, collision, or determinism decision reads it.

Decision: [[file:DECISIONS.md::Background composition][Background composition]].
Plan: [[file:plans/3B-scrolling-background.md][3B implementation steps]].

- [ ] **3B.1 Atlas and data model.** Define the 16-panel atlas, its frame
      layout, the two layer configurations, and the seeded selection of panel
      and orientation.
- [ ] **3B.2 Seeded per-level composition.** Draw a cohesive panel subset from
      the level seed and build a periodic tile pattern; same seed, same pattern.
- [ ] **3B.3 Scroll and animate.** Scroll the back layer slowly and the front
      layer quickly, wrap the pattern vertically, and loop each panel through

- [ ] **3B.4 State integration.** Advance scroll and animation in the menu and

- [ ] **3B.5 Verify.** Frame time, texture lifetime, same-seed pattern, no

** Phase 5B — Player survival and damage

Inserted after Phase 5 and before Phase 6. This is the missing fail state: health,
contact damage, hazards, invulnerability, and a reachable game over. Phase 6's
Clostridium hazard and Phase 8's bosses both assume it.

Decision: [[file:DECISIONS.md::Player survival and damage][Player survival and damage]].

- [ ] Add player health with variable damage: maximum, current, and a
      bottom-left health bar.
- [ ] Make bacteria contact mutual: the bacterium damages the player and takes
      contact damage itself, but is not destroyed outright by the contact. It
      dies only if the contact damage brings its health to zero.
- [ ] Give the player a short global invulnerability window after a hit, with
      visible feedback, so one contact cannot drain several hits in a frame.
- [ ] Route hazard damage through the same player-damage path. Phase 6's spore
      hazard is the first consumer.
- [ ] Make `.Game_Over` reachable at zero health and offer **Retry level** and
      **Restart run**.
- [ ] Retry re-initializes the current level from the run seed and level number.
      Restart re-initializes from level 1 with the run seed. Both reset player
      health, bullets, hazards, and the return roster deliberately.
- [ ] Decide and record whether health carries between levels or is restored on
      advance; keep the policy in one place.
- [ ] Keep boss and hazard balance out of this phase; it only builds the damage
      path and the fail state.

*** Remaining roster backlog evaluation

Evaluate each candidate by what the current roster does not cover.

| Candidate | Role | Required behaviors | Overlap risk | Promote when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salmonella | Fast, erratic attacker | High-speed movement, irregular pathing | Overlaps E. coli's fast pressure | E. coli alone makes levels feel samey |
| Helicobacter | Corrosive area control | Lingering ground effect, contact damage | Overlaps Clostridium's hazard | The spore hazard works and a second hazard type is wanted |
| Vibrio | Splitting threat | On-death split into smaller units | Overlaps wave accounting and caps | Entity caps and wave resolution handle splits cleanly |
| Bacillus | Spore armor | Temporary armored phase, then vulnerability | Overlaps Klebsiella's durability | Armor and damage reduction are shared traits |
| Neisseria | Paired attackers | Pair relation, synchronized dive | Overlaps Strep's swarm | The relation table and group behaviors exist |
| Enterococcus | Support / buff spreader | Aura that benefits allies | Overlaps Klebsiella's aura | Auras are a reusable shared behavior |
| Borrelia | Evasive attacker | Sidestep or dodge behavior | Overlaps Pseudomonas's movement | Movement behaviors are compositional |
| Mycobacterium | Slow, very durable | High health, slow advance | Overlaps Klebsiella's tank role | Klebsiella proves the protector distinction |
| Chlamydia | Hidden / delayed threat | Spawns later from an existing enemy | Overlaps lifecycle behaviors | Lifecycle composition is stable |

Promote a candidate only when its "promote when" condition is true and its role
adds a decision the current roster lacks.
